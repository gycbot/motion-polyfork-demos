# Viral Chinese copy (title & contrast subset)

## Goal
Produce scroll-stopping **titles/hooks** and contrast frames without illegal or overpromise claims.

## Source modules (vendor)
Prefer these folders under `viral-copywriting-skill/` when present:
- `cognitive-contrast` — bad→good contrast
- `news-editorial-title` — news-style authority titles
- `practical-tip-title` — tip/list titles
- `friend-chat-title` — conversational titles
- `copywriting-four-step-system` — structure/diagnosis only

Also use team `hook-writer` / `contrarian-and-opinion` patterns from social-media-skills when available.

## Steps
1. Extract the one true benefit or tension from the brief (must be true to the content).
2. Generate **3–5** title variants across at least two of the modules above.
3. Add one **contrarian / conflict** angle that stays factual.
4. Compliance self-check (fail → rewrite):
   - No income/follower guarantees
   - No medical/finance absolute claims
   - No platform-rule evasion wording
   - Referral lines: official job links + codes only; no "free offer" bait
5. Hand off chosen title + runner-ups + the check result with the script/card.

## Do not
Auto-load fear-appeal / price-anchor sales funnel into growth posts unless the owner explicitly asked for sales copy.
